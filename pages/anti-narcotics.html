    CNSA 1997 Categories

    • Class A (Under 100 grams) Applies to minor quantities. Eligible for bail in most cases. Sentence up to 2 years.
    • Class B (100 to 1000 grams) Medium quantity. Bail depends on discretion and defense grounds. Sentence up to 7 years.
    • Class C (Exceeds 1000 grams) Large quantity. Under Prohibitory Clause of Section 497 CrPC. Extremely difficult bail. Life imprisonment / Capital punishment.

    Your Constitutional Rights

    ⚖️

    Right to Counsel

    You have the right to be represented by a lawyer immediately upon arrest or inquiry invitation by ANF or Police.

    🛡️

    Search Warrant Checks

    Private house searches must follow strict procedural codes under Section 103 CrPC, requiring witnesses.

    📝

    FIR Copy

    The accused is constitutionally entitled to receive a complete copy of the First Information Report (FIR) and recovery memo.
