1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • To remember language and user preferences.
  • To analyze website traffic and visitor behavior.

Some third-party services integrated into our website may use their own cookies, including analytics and social media tools.

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.
